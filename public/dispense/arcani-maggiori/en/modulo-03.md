- Devil (XV) and Tower (XVI): Shadow, collapse and liberation.
- Star, Moon and Sun: hope, the unconscious and visible clarity.
- Judgement (XX) and World (XXI): awakening and final triumph.

## The Third Septenary: Spiritual Mastery and Fulfilment (XV – XXI)

In the third septenary the individual confronts the great universal forces: overcomes the illusions of the Shadow, tears down false certainties and realises inner and outer potential.

### 15. The Devil (XV) – Shadow, Matter and the Fascination of Power

- **Symbolism and essence**: winged creature above two figures loosely but willingly bound.
- **Deeper meaning**: confrontation with the Shadow, attachment to matter, passions, money, dependencies, false powers. Recognising unconscious desires in order to regain control.
- **In a predictive key**: strong passions, power or manipulation dynamics, material profit; need to free oneself from toxic bonds.

### 16. The Tower (XVI) – Lightning, Collapse and Liberation

- **Symbolism and essence**: tower struck by lightning; crown flying off; figures falling amid sparks.
- **Deeper meaning**: breaking of false certainties, collapse of structures no longer true, cathartic liberation through crisis.
- **In a predictive key**: drastic and unexpected change that breaks stagnation and frees new energies.

### 17. The Star (XVII) – Hope, Inspiration and Healing

- **Symbolism and essence**: nude figure pouring life-water on earth and water, under radiant stars.
- **Deeper meaning**: celestial guidance, trust, clarity of intent, inspiration, healing after the Tower.
- **In a predictive key**: regained serenity, favourable prospects, creative success, protection, confirmation of the path.

### 18. The Moon (XVIII) – Unconscious, Illusions and the Night Path

- **Symbolism and essence**: full Moon, crayfish from the waters, wolves and distant towers.
- **Deeper meaning**: realm of the unconscious, dreams, ancestral fears, visceral intuition, illusions and mists to cross with inner light.
- **In a predictive key**: uncertainty, secrets, misunderstandings; proceed by listening to instinct without anxious paralysis.

### 19. The Sun (XIX) – Clarity, Joy and Visible Realisation

- **Symbolism and essence**: radiant Sun; joyful children in a flowering meadow.
- **Deeper meaning**: unveiled truth, joy, transparency, human warmth, public success, conscious vitality.
- **In a predictive key**: success, clarity in relationships, fortunate agreements, vitality, triumph of a project brought into the light.

### 20. Judgement (XX) – Call, Awakening and Renewal

- **Symbolism and essence**: angel with trumpet; figures rising from tombs, arms open.
- **Deeper meaning**: call to awareness, awakening, forgiveness, liberation from limiting judgements.
- **In a predictive key**: decisive turn, important news, reconciliation that renews existence.

### 21. The World (XXI) – Fulfilment and the Perfect Circle

- **Symbolism and essence**: dancing figure in a leafy mandorla, surrounded by the four living beings (Bull, Lion, Eagle, Angel).
- **Deeper meaning**: Completion of the Work, integration of the septenaries, realisation and universal harmony.
- **In a predictive key**: total success, triumphant close of a cycle, international travel, maximum expansion.

### Didactic Summary of Module 3

1. Devil (XV) and Tower (XVI) break material bonds.
2. From the Star (XVII) and the Moon (XVIII) one reaches the Sun (XIX).
3. Judgement’s call to awakening (XX) leads to fulfilment and the final triumph of the World (XXI).
