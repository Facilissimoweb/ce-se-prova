## Teaching Handout · Major Arcana (First Step)

The Major Arcana are not tools of mechanical fortune-telling, but a **structured map** of human experience, psyche, and evolutionary cycles. This handout opens Tarot Italia’s First Step: from the architecture of the Three Septenaries to the grounding of the First Septenary, from The Fool to The Chariot.

## 1. Teaching Architecture and the Map of the Three Septenaries

The deck unfolds across **twenty-two trumps** (from card 0 to XXI), organised into three great cycles of inner growth and outer manifestation. Each septenary answers a precise phase of individual evolution, marking the passage from matter to spirit.

### Map of the Three Cycles

- **First Septenary (0 – VII)** — Formation of identity, grounding in the physical world, concrete action and the conquest of direction.
- **Second Septenary (VIII – XIV)** — Ethical trial, moral maturation, interiorisation, transition and the search for inner balance.
- **Third Septenary (XV – XXI)** — Spiritual mastery, confrontation with the Shadow, liberation from bonds and fulfilment in the world.

## 2. The First Septenary: Formation of Identity (0 – VII)

The first cycle is the phase of grounding and of building the conscious personality. Through these first eight cards, the individual learns to handle material resources, creativity, authority, ethical guidance and choice, up to the triumphant affirmation of disciplined will.

### 0. The Fool – The Unconditioned Beginning

- **Symbolism and essence**: The Fool (0) stands at the threshold outside sequential numbering; a step at the precipice; absolute freedom. It precedes every scheme, rule or mental superstructure: pure energy before any material crystallisation.
- **Deeper meaning**: genuine impetus, unconditional openness to the new, courage to loosen conditioning, trust in the path. Every authentic beginning requires the risk of the unknown and shedding superfluous protections.
- **In a predictive key**: sudden change of course, imminent departure, leap of trust, unexpected novelty. It asks for audacity, spontaneity and a pioneering instinct, without paralysing logical guarantees.

### The Creative Dyad: The Magician (I) and The High Priestess (II)

The initial dynamics of the manifested path divide between visible action and inner gestation.

### 1. The Magician (I) – Initiative and Talent at Work

- **Symbolism and essence**: At the head of the numbering, the Magician (or Juggler) lays out on the table all the primary tools (wands, cups, swords and pentacles). He is master of operative resources and of the material potentials needed to begin at once.
- **Deeper meaning**: immediate initiative, pure talent, dexterity, activating processes with what is already available, without waiting for ideal conditions.
- **In a predictive key**: immediate practical action; “get your hands dirty”, use practical skills and bring a project or action into form within the very first days.

### 2. The High Priestess (II) – Gestation and the Wisdom of Silence

- **Symbolism and essence**: Seated still and veiled between the temple pillars, with the sacred book closed or half-open; she guards subtle knowledge without acting outwardly.
- **Deeper meaning**: silent gestation, truth intuited before it is spoken, wisdom of timing, fertile waiting. Every true creation needs gathering-in and inner maturation.
- **In a predictive key**: not the moment to act or expose ideas; keep, study, reflect, research and let events ripen in depth.

### From Blossoming to Conquest (III – VII)

The progression leads from creative flowering to acceleration and the victory of disciplined will.

### 3. The Empress (III) – Creative Flowering and Abundance

- **Symbolism and essence**: sensitive flowering, generative abundance, the capacity to give form and life to ideas with expressiveness, communication and fertile care of projects.
- **Deeper meaning**: lush vitality, unconstrained creative expression, generative power of the word; harmony that turns impulse into rich manifestation.
- **In a predictive key**: prosperous expansion, successful agreements, flowering of new ideas, fecundity in projects, great relational harmony.

### 4. The Emperor (IV) – Structural Stability and Method

- **Symbolism and essence**: authority and concreteness; stability, rigorous method, solid boundaries, organised management of the material world.
- **Deeper meaning**: wise authority, protection, governing reality without hesitation; structural strength to give duration and clear boundaries to what has been created.
- **In a predictive key**: consolidation of work or financial projects, solid foundations, an authoritative or protective reference, methodical and effective management of matter.

### 5. The Hierophant (V) – The Ethical Pact and Respectable Guidance

- **Symbolism and essence**: spiritual or professional guidance; a teacher who transmits shared values and facilitates adherence to coded rules and principles.
- **Deeper meaning**: ethical pact, transmission of wisdom, illuminated mediation; alliance between individual aspirations and tradition or social structure.
- **In a predictive key**: clear and transparent contracts, successful mediations, authoritative counsel, alliances based on trust, respect for ethical norms.

### 6. The Lovers (VI) – The Fundamental Crossroads and the Heart’s Choice

- **Symbolism and essence**: the human figure at the fork, facing encounter, affection and the responsibility of decision.
- **Deeper meaning**: conscious choice of the heart and responsibility toward one’s emotional and existential direction; opting with conviction for one road while excluding the others.
- **In a predictive key**: unavoidable sentimental or professional decisions; a clear love or values choice; authentic alignment with deep desires.

### 7. The Chariot (VII) – Coordinated Action and Disciplined Victory

- **Symbolism and essence**: the commander guides the triumphal Chariot and masters the opposing forces that move the vehicle.
- **Deeper meaning**: coordinated action, wise control of energetic polarities, victory through determination and rigour.
- **In a predictive key**: rapid unblocking of stalled negotiations or situations, journeys, material moves, decisive and triumphant acceleration toward goals.

### Didactic Summary of Module 1

1. The Fool (0) guarantees original freedom and unconditional openness to change.
2. The Magician (I) / High Priestess (II) polarity establishes the alternation between visible action and intuitive gestation.
3. From Empress (III) and Emperor (IV) one reaches the Hierophant’s ethical guidance (V), the Lovers’ choice (VI) and the Chariot’s drive (VII).

### Practical Exercises for the First Septenary

- **Exercise 1 (The Fool and the blank page)**: identify a postponed project and write the minimum courageous action to take today, without planning every detail.
- **Exercise 2 (Magician vs High Priestess)**: facing a doubt, draw a card. Magician → practical action within 24 hours; High Priestess → three days of silence and listening before deciding.
- **Exercise 3 (Two-card reading)**: combine The Chariot with a previous trump to interpret an unexpected unblock in work or personal life.
