## Final Practical Workshop and Skills Check

This section turns the theoretical framing of the Three Septenaries into **active competence**, to verify assimilation and use the cards in practical consultation.

## Cycle Self-Assessment Test

Answer the guiding questions to see which septenary resonates most often in your situation (or the querent’s).

### 1. Starting Phase — First Septenary (0 – VII)

Is a new beginning underway, a search for direction, or the laying of practical project foundations? If so, analyse the energy of the Fool, the Magician and the Chariot.

### 2. Choice and Elaboration Phase — Second Septenary (VIII – XIV)

Are blocks, forced pauses, norms to respect or radical transformations being faced? If so, deepen the lesson of the Hermit, the Hanged One or Death.

### 3. Turning Point and Realisation Phase — Third Septenary (XV – XXI)

Are limiting patterns being cut, Shadow aspects faced, or the fruits of a complete path gathered? If so, observe the impact of the Tower, the Sun or the World.

## Practical Reading Exercises

### Exercise A — The tris of the septenaries

Draw one card at random from the deck. Recognise which of the three septenaries it belongs to and articulate aloud the evolutionary lesson it expresses for the situation under study.

### Exercise B — The three-phase spread

Perform a three-card spread, one card per septenary:

1. **First card (1st Septenary)** — starting point and immediate practical action.
2. **Second card (2nd Septenary)** — inner knot or ethical trial to overcome.
3. **Third card (3rd Septenary)** — perspective of unblock and final evolutionary direction.

## Closing the First Step

After self-assessment and exercises, review Trumps 0–XXI with the **learning Seal** (flashcards) and mark completed modules in your Reserved Area.
