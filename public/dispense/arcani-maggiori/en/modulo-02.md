## The Second Septenary: Ethical Trial and Interiorisation (VIII – XIV)

If the first septenary builds outer identity, the second shifts the axis inward: moral maturation, confrontation with limits, balance and deep transformation.

### 8. Justice (VIII) – Order, Measure and Cause-Effect

- **Symbolism and essence**: regal figure on a throne, vertical sword and balanced scales; cosmic order, impartiality and the linearity of consequences.
- **Deeper meaning**: responsibility, intellectual honesty, balance, ethical rigour. Every action generates consequence; mental clarity guides emotion.
- **In a predictive key**: legal or bureaucratic matters, fair contracts, need to put things in order, a deserved and objective response.

### 9. The Hermit (IX) – Inner Light and Time for Research

- **Symbolism and essence**: sage in a cloak, lantern in the night; protects and illuminates the path.
- **Deeper meaning**: gathering-in, regenerating solitude, search for inner truth, prudence; temporary distance from noise to find authentic answers.
- **In a predictive key**: slowing down, study, introspection, caution and reserve, without rushing time.

### 10. Wheel of Fortune (X) – Cycle, Change and the Unexpected

- **Symbolism and essence**: a wheel in motion and figures in evolution; life cycles and the mutability of circumstances.
- **Deeper meaning**: acceptance of change, cyclicality, unblocking stagnation; adapting with flexibility.
- **In a predictive key**: sudden turn, plot twist, new cycle, rapid overturning of a blocked situation.

### 11. Strength (XI) – Instinctive Mastery and Gentle Courage

- **Symbolism and essence**: a woman gently opens or closes a lion’s jaws, without violence.
- **Deeper meaning**: mastery of passions through awareness, inner courage, patience, wise management of energy; integrating impulses rather than repressing them.
- **In a predictive key**: overcoming a complex trial with determination and self-control; harmony within tensions.

### 12. The Hanged One (XII) – Voluntary Pause and Change of Perspective

- **Symbolism and essence**: figure upside down, serene; energy flows upward.
- **Deeper meaning**: necessary pause, sacrifice as “making sacred”, releasing rigid control, a reversal of gaze.
- **In a predictive key**: a block or wait useful for reconsidering priorities, letting go of haste and accepting the pause.

### 13. The Nameless Arcana / Death (XIII) – Transmutation and Clean Cut

- **Symbolism and essence**: a skeleton that reaps; shoots emerge from the ground; pruning indispensable to life.
- **Deeper meaning**: radical end of a cycle, detachment from what is obsolete, transformation and rebirth to make room for the new.
- **In a predictive key**: clean closure and drastic change; if welcomed without resistance, it opens to total renewal.

### 14. Temperance (XIV) – Daily Alchemy and Moderation

- **Symbolism and essence**: winged figure pouring liquid between two vessels; continuous flow of harmony.
- **Deeper meaning**: measure, healing, alchemical patience, reconciliation, compromise and inner peace.
- **In a predictive key**: peaceful resolution, effective care, fluid recovery times, a stabilising phase where cooperation reigns.

### Didactic Summary of Module 2

1. From Justice (VIII) to the Hermit (IX): moral maturation and listening to conscience.
2. Wheel (X) and Strength (XI): acceptance of change and wise mastery of energy.
3. Hanged One (XII), Death (XIII) and Temperance (XIV): emotional and ethical transition toward the third septenary.
